# Session 9 - The Sacked Cloister, Hospital of Spores, and The Basalt March

**Outline:** Freshly returned to Toril from the nightmare wastes of Mal'Goroth, the newly minted Level 5 party stands amidst the smoking ash and slaughtered defenders of Dragon's Rest. Following an urgent clue and a bioluminescent trail of purple spores into the subterranean depths of Seagrow Caves, the heroes discover that the surviving cloister residents have taken refuge with Sovereign Sinensa and the myconids. In this fungal field hospital, the party reunites with Tarak, Myla, and Blepp, receiving emotional blessings, vital boons, and Myla's anti-lightning Galvanic Ley-Grounder. Marching out onto the storm-lashed basalt spires, the party navigates a gauntlet of hilarious, fanatical Kobold Camps—testing their newly awakened Level 5 powers through explosive combat, fake-god comedy, and tactical mutinies—uncovering proof that Sparkrender lied about Sharruth and seeks the ancient Draconic Star-Atlas to claim godhood in the lost lands across the sea!

---

## Moments

### Moment 1 (The Charred Altar & The Trail of Spores)
The party investigates the smoking ruins of Dragon's Rest. Bodies of fallen cloister defenders lie among scorched timbers, and the Temple of Bahamut has been desecrated by blue dragon lightning. Near the fractured central pool, Eflein notices glowing purple spore dust pressed into the soot leading down toward the southern cliff path to Seagrow Caves, accompanied by a hasty charcoal scrawl on a shattered bakery board: **"SEAGROW... RETREAT BELOW..."**
* **Enemies:** None (investigation, atmosphere, and uncovering the blue lightning scorch marks).
* **Possible Loot:** Residual holy incense, scorched kobold spear, unburned scroll of *Feather Fall*.
* **If/then:**
  * **If the party attempts to march directly toward the Clifftop Observatory first:** Howling gale-force lightning squalls and locked iron mountain portcullises block the low pass, forcing them to regroup with the survivors in the caves and secure Myla's countermeasures.
  * **If Father Flubs offers prayers for the fallen kobolds:** Thurible's hearth flares with warm amber embers, granting the party a feeling of solemn resolve.

---

### Moment 2 (The Hospital of Spores & The Fulfilling Send-Off)
Entering Seagrow Caves, the party finds the tranquil fungal garden transformed into a bustling, emotional refugee camp and field hospital. Sovereign Sinensa and adult myconids pulse soothing rapport spores over dozens of wounded kobolds. Tarak is frantically bandaging wounds, while Blepp the baker stirs mushroom broth with his lucky dagger, weeping over fallen kitchen friends Agga and Frub. Before the heroes depart for the mountain, the survivors gather for an emotionally fulfilling send-off packed with heart, humor, and vital boons:
* **Myla's Vulnerable Plea & The Pylon:** Myla presents the heavy copper **Galvanic Ley-Grounder ("Spark-Tamer" Pylon)**. Pushing up her welding goggles, she tearfully confesses that her traitorous brothers, **Mek and Minn**, were duped by Sparkrender's promises of dragon wings: *"Kick their teeth in if you have to, but please... they're my idiot brothers. Bring them home to me alive."*
* **Tarak's Quiet Atonement:** Tarak hands Father Flubs a vial of **Heartcap Draught** (restores 2d6+4 HP and cures poison/disease) and nods grimly to Varnoth: *"Dragon's Rest isn't stone walls. It's the people who believe second chances are worth dying for. Come back in one piece, Commander—the tomatoes won't plant themselves."*
* **Sovereign Sinensa's Telepathic Blessing:** The towering myconid exhales a golden cloud of communion spores: *« You healed our rot. You are our family. Go with our roots at your backs. »* Every party member gains **+5 Temporary Hit Points** for the march!
* **Thurible's Good-Luck Ribbon:** Little bandaged kobold children scurry forward, giggling through tears as they tie a braided red-and-gold flower ribbon around Thurible's massive neck: *"Bring Elder Runara home, big fire-snake... we saved extra honey-buns for her!"*
* **Enemies:** None (heartwarming roleplay, farewells, and gear prep).
* **Possible Loot / Boons:** **The Galvanic Ley-Grounder Pylon** (Myla), **Heartcap Draught** (Tarak), **+5 Temp HP Blessing** (Sinensa), 2x Potions of Healing, 4x Bowls of Heartcap Broth.
* **If/then:**
  * **If Sylvar or Flubs comforts Blepp:** The young kobold presses a freshly baked crust of "luck-bread" into their hands (+1 to one d20 roll today).
  * **If Eflein promises Myla he will spare Mek and Minn:** Myla gives him a set of insulated copper spark-gloves (+1 to Dex saves vs lightning).

---

### Moment 3 (The Basalt March & Camp Dragon-Fang — The Fake-Snout Outpost)
Stepping out of the caves, the party ascends the steep, volcanic basalt switchbacks toward the mountain heights. Perched at a narrow choke-point along the ridge sits **Camp Dragon-Fang**—a makeshift barricade of sharpened logs manned by 5 fanatical Kobold Cultists and their self-important sergeant, **Griblet**, who struts around wearing a painted cardboard-and-tin dragon snout and roasting coastal crabs over a bonfire!
* **Enemies:** 1x Winged Kobold Sergeant Griblet (AC 13, HP 16, Sling +4, Fire Pot), 5x Kobold Cultists (AC 12, HP 7, Daggers +4, Pack Tactics).
* **Possible Loot:** 2x Ceramic Fire Pots (2d6 fire grenade, 10-ft radius), roasted spicy sea-crabs (delicious!), 14 gp, 25 sp, Griblet’s crude hand-drawn map of the ridge.
* **The 3 Fun Encounter Approaches:**
  * **Option A — The Level 5 Combat Flex:** Eflein drops a *Shatter* or 3rd-level evocation spell; Varnoth charges in with her brand-new **Extra Attack**; Sylvar triggers **Psychic Blades** (+3d6). The players get to steamroll the outpost in 1–2 rounds, laughing as kobolds scatter and tumble down the gravel slopes!
  * **Option B — The Fake Dragon Messiah Routine (Sylvar & Thurible Show):** Sylvar steps forward with high Performance/Deception, presenting the 12-foot, glowing lava snake Thurible as *« The Living Emissary of Primordial Flame sent to test your loyalty! »* Thurible flares his molten scales with a Nat 20-level purr. Griblet and the cultists instantly drop to their knees, weeping and offering their roasted crabs and shiny trinkets!
  * **Option C — The Interrogation & The Propaganda Clue:** Searching the camp or shaking down Griblet reveals a crumpled scrap of parchment written in Draconic:  
    > *"Orders from the Summit: Hold the ridge! The Draconic Star-Atlas is almost calibrated! Once Lord Sparkrender claims the coordinates to the Great Wyrm-Lands across the sea, he will leave this miserable rock behind and take his true chosen to become gods! (Scrawled below in shaky handwriting: 'Wait... why does the manifest say only ONE passenger?')"*  
    This seeds the realization: **Sparkrender is abandoning the cultists and never cared about Sharruth!**

### Moment 4 (The Upper Perch — The Catapult & The Cultist Mutiny)
Just below the final overlook (Area D1), the trail opens onto a fortified stone ledge hanging 200 feet over the roaring sea. Here, a heavily armed watch-post stands equipped with a crude, stone-throwing catapult loaded with fire pots. Guarding it are 6 Kobold skirmishers and 1 Guard Drake—and the kobolds are actively in the middle of a screaming, hilarious argument!
* **The Drama:** Half the squad is terrified because Sparkrender shocked them with blue lightning when they asked for food; the other half are arguing about why Sparkrender is obsessed with a giant floor map instead of waking Sharruth!
* **Enemies:** 1x Guard Drake (AC 14, HP 52, Bite +5 hit 1d10+3, Tail +5), 6x Kobold Skirmishers (AC 12, HP 7).
* **Possible Loot:** Catapult winch gears, 2x Spitfire Flasks, 1x Potion of Healing, 22 gp.
* **The 3 Encounter Approaches:**
  * **Option A — The Social Mutiny (DC 11 Persuasion):** Father Flubs or Sylvar calls out to the bickering guards, revealing that Sparkrender called them "filthy ditch-rats" and has no intention of sharing power. The disillusioned kobolds stage an immediate mutiny—tying up the Guard Drake, handing over the catapult's Spitfire Flasks, and pointing out the safest climb to the roof!
  * **Option B — The Catapult Heist / Tactical Ambush:** Eflein slips behind the catapult using *Misty Step*, Varnoth uses an Athletics check to violently kick the catapult off the cliff into the sea below, and Thurible unleashes a fire-whip sweep.
  * **Option C — The Sister's Goggles:** Showing Myla's welding goggles instantly stuns the kobolds into silence: *"You came from the caves?! Myla is still alive?!"*

---

### Moment 5 (Area D1 — The Overlook & Starlight Bridge to the Climax)
Reaching the top of the ridge, the party stands at Area D1. A 60-foot sheer drop separates the trail from the massive brass doors of the Clifftop Observatory. 
* **The Interaction:** General Varnoth points to the carved bronze dragon statue at the cliff edge: *"The slot. Put the Moonstone Key in!"*
* **The Starlight Bridge:** Sliding the Moonstone Key into the statue causes a humming, translucent bridge of solid starlight to solidify across the chasm! Smeared across the starlight are fresh blood drops and scorched bronze scales—Elder Runara was dragged across this bridge within the last hour.
* **The Threshold:** Crossing the bridge, the party kicks open the heavy doors, stepping into the Observatory rotunda where the grand confrontation begins!

## Character Moments

* **Eflein:** Grappling with the fact that he holds **Astalagan's Bronze Scale**—the final missing key Sparkrender needs to unlock the Star-Atlas. Feeling the deep emotional connection to his wife Lianna, knowing she walked this exact stone floor months ago and unlocked the very same map. Testing his newly unlocked Level 5 evocation spells (*Fireball*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Counterspell*) and his Vecna-warped arm.
* **Sylvar Link:** Leading the charge with acoustic blindsight across the windy cliffs; turning potential massacres into hilarious, face-saving kobold surrenders using his charisma, Lyra, and tambourine rhythms; comforting the wounded cloister survivors before the march.
* **Father Flubs & Thurible:** Serving as the beloved heart of the cloister. Riding Thurible adorned with the children's flower ribbon; channeling divine Life domain spells to heal the wounded; outsmarting the cultists by showing that Bahamut's mercy is real while Sparkrender's promises are poison.
* **General Varnoth:** Slipping seamlessly into frontline military commander mode. Checking weapons, holding tactical formations, protecting the squishier spellcasters on the cliff edges, and testing her brand-new **Extra Attack** and bone-cleaver strikes.

## Location Details

* **The Sacked Cloister of Dragon's Rest:** A grim graveyard of burnt cedar, shattered white marble, and weeping smoke. The sacred spring is choked with gray soot, and the quiet sanctuary where they once danced is silent.
* **Seagrow Caves (Hospital of Spores):** Densely humid and cool, filled with glowing turquoise and violet bioluminescent mushrooms. Smells of crushed sage, iodine, and warm mushroom broth. The rhythmic pulsing of myconid spores fills the cavern air with warmth and peace.
* **The Basalt Switchbacks:** Jagged, obsidian-black cliffs jutting straight out of the crashing ocean. Thick clouds of static electricity roll down from the summit, making hair stand on end and metal weapons hum with blue sparks.
* **Camp Dragon-Fang & The Upper Perch:** Crude barricades lashed with leather, smelling of woodsmoke and roasted crabs, decorated with chalk-drawn dragon runes and fake wings.
* **Area D1 (The Overlook & Starlight Bridge):** A breathtaking precipice overlooking the crashing waves 300 feet below, bridged only by solid, shimmering moonlight.

## People

* **Sovereign Sinensa:** 8-foot-tall myconid ruler; deeply grateful to the heroes for curing her in Chapter 2, using her colony's entire spore medicine reserves to save the kobold refugees.
* **Tarak:** Ex-assassin herbalist; exhausted, blood-spattered, driven by an oath never to let another innocent die under his care.
* **Blepp:** Cheerful baker turned broken survivor; clutching his "lucky dagger," desperate to feed anyone who can bring Elder Runara home.
* **Myla "Goggles":** Fierce winged kobold artificer; creator of the Galvanic Ley-Grounder Pylon, terrified for her wayward brothers Mek and Minn.
* **Sergeant Griblet:** Comedic kobold cultist captain in a painted tin snout, desperately clinging to his dreams of dragon wings.
* **Mek & Minn:** Myla's gullible brothers waiting inside the rotunda, on the verge of realizing their "dragon god" lied to them.
* **Elder Runara:** Adult Bronze Dragon in chains; held as the bleeding magical conduit over the Draconic Star-Atlas.
* **Sparkrender (The Apex Blue Wyrmling):** Vain, lightning-charged tyrant perched on the telescope dome, obsessed with using the Star-Atlas to reach the Lost Dragon Lands and become a god.

---

## Plotlines

* **The Stolen Scales:** Sparkrender already possesses **Clyssavar's Silver Scale** (stolen from the *Compass Rose*) and **Turadaer's Gold Scale** (taken from the observatory vault). **Astalagan's Bronze Scale** is currently in Eflein's coat pocket—the ultimate missing trigger!
* **The Star-Atlas Secret (Not Sharruth!):** Sparkrender never cared about freeing the ancient red dragon Sharruth; he is using the three scales and Runara's blood to decode the **Draconic Star-Atlas** to sail across the sea to the **Lost Lands of the Dragons (The Wyrm-Cradle)** to seize primordial creator-magic and become a dragon god!
* **The Siblings' Redemption:** Fulfilling Myla's plea by capturing or talking down Mek and Minn instead of slaughtering them.
* **The Archmage's Footsteps:** Eflein and Sylvar realize the Star-Atlas is the exact apparatus Lianna and Lyra decoded 8 months ago.

## Cliffhanger & The Apex Confrontation

Standing on the starlight bridge, the party kicks open the heavy brass doors of the Clifftop Observatory rotunda. The air inside smells of scorched ozone and ancient dust. Above, through the shattered glass dome, blue lightning chains across the brass arches, illuminating a colossal, glowing map carved into the obsidian floor—**The Draconic Star-Atlas**!

Chained across the center lies Elder Runara, her bronze scales bleeding into the glowing leylines. Floating in mid-air above the altar are the **Gold** and **Silver** scales. A third socket—shaped for the Bronze Scale—sits empty.

Perched atop the shattered brass telescope arch, his azure scales gleaming as sparks dance along his horns, **Sparkrender** looks down and hisses:

> *"Look at you. Crawling up my mountain like ants after a rainstorm. Did that old cow Runara whisper fairy tales into your ears? Did she tell you I was going to crack the earth and wake up **Sharruth**?*
> 
> *(He snorts, spitting a shower of sparks onto the stone)*  
> 
> *"Sharruth! A bloated, brainless fossil rotting in magma! Why in the Nine Hells would I wake an ancient red worm, just to bow at his tail and fight for scraps?! I am a prince of storm and sky! I bow to NO ONE!*
> 
> *Look at the floor beneath your filthy boots, mortals. The ancients didn't build this tower to watch the stars—they built it to MAP THEM! The **Draconic Star-Atlas**! The hidden nautical ley-path to the Lost Primordial Lands across the sea... where draconic gods once walked, and fonts of raw, uncorrupted creator-magic still bleed into the earth!*
> 
> *(He glances down at the worshipful, trembling cultist kobolds around the pillars, grinning with cruelty)*  
> 
> *"These pathetic little wretches thought I was going to share it with them. 'Help me take the cloister, little lizards, and I’ll turn you into winged dragon-lords!' Ha! As if filthy ditch-rats could ever wear the mantle of dragons! They were just hands to turn the wrenches and dig the trenches.*
> 
> *(His massive blue head swoops down, hovering inches from Eflein, his sulfurous breath hot and charged with static)*  
> 
> *"I ripped Dragon's Rest to splinters looking for it. I tore their archives apart. I bled Runara until her voice gave out... and all along, the wizard had it tucked in his coat.*
> 
> *The Gold is mine. The Silver is mine. And now, you bring me **Astalagan’s Bronze Scale** on a silver platter.*
> 
> *Hand over the scale, boy. Place it in the altar, and I might let you live long enough to watch me step into the heavens as a god."*
