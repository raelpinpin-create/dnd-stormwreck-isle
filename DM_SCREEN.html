⚖️ Skill Checks vs. Saving Throws Breakdown

DM Core Rules

🎯 When to call for a SKILL CHECK:

Proactive action with chance of failure: climbing a cliff (Athletics), searching a room (Investigation), convincing an NPC (Persuasion).

🛡️ When to call for a SAVING THROW:

Reactive defense against external threat: dodging lightning (DEX Save), resisting toxic fumes (CON Save), resisting charm (WIS Save).

🤝 Daggerheart Help System (2 Tokens/Rest):

Free call-out within 30 ft. Spend 1 token to roll +1d6 Advantage Die for ally's d20 roll. Multiple allies can help; acting player picks the highest single 1d6 result!

💥 Brutal Crits & Environmental Combos:

Nat 20: Max Base Damage + 1 Normal Damage Roll + Modifiers. Lightning + Water: Arcs +1d4 Shock damage. Ice + Impact: Shatters +1d4 Cold Shrapnel.

💰 Currency Economy (Standard 5e: 10 SP = 1 GP):

10 Silver pieces equals 1 Gold piece (100 Copper). When silver reaches 0 and decreases, 1 Gold is automatically converted into 10 Silver on all character cards.
