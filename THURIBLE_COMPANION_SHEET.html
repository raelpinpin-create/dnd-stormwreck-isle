🔥 Elemental Mount Traits & Sacred Rider Bond
🐍🏇 Mount of the Serpent Rider: Thurible is a Large battle mount ridden by Father Flubs! While mounted, Flubs gains elevated tactical vantage, 30 ft movement (including 30 ft climbing up sheer basalt cliffs and 30 ft swimming through heated coastal waters), and is shielded from volcanic heat and freezing cold through their sacred bond.
🔥 Living Hearth (Aura of Volcanic Radiance): Thurible sheds soothing warm bright light in a 10-ft radius and dim light for 10 ft. Father Flubs and all allies within 10 ft gain complete IMMUNITY TO COLD DAMAGE and immunity to extreme cold weather & freezing terrain!
🍾 Sacred Censer Bond: Coils comfortably around Father Flubs' holy symbol jug (The Vintage of the Dawn Watch) or carries him majestically upon its molten back. Understands Ignan and Common.
👁️ Senses & Movement: Blindsight 10 ft, Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 11. Slithers up sheer walls (Climb 30 ft) and navigates volcanic waters (Swim 30 ft).
