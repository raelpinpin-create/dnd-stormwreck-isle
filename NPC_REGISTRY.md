## 🐉 Key Inhabitants of Dragon's Rest

### 🐉 Elder Runara (Leader of Dragon's Rest)
* **True Form:** Adult Bronze Dragon in human guise (wise, peaceful, protective).
* **Appearance:** Elderly human woman with weathered brown skin, white braided hair, and kind hazel eyes in a simple white robe.
* **Role & Secret:** Secretly an ancient bronze dragon dedicated to breaking the cycle of dragon feuds by offering sanctuary to mortals and lost souls.
* **Relationships:**
  * **Player Character:** Welcomes the Wizard to the sanctuary; tests their heart before revealing the location of the Arcane Observatory.
  * **Cloister Hermits:** Loving maternal leader to the kobolds, Tarak, and Varnoth.
* **DM Roleplay Guide:** Serene, soft-spoken, deeply gentle yet radiating underlying ancient power. Never uses violence unless innocent lives are threatened.

### 🌿 Tarak (The Herbalist & Botanist)
* **Appearance:** Middle-aged human with tanned freckled skin, graying auburn hair/beard, and faded neck tattoos.
* **Role:** Cloister botanist & potion maker. Former assassin/poisoner for the *Gilded Gallows* guild.
* **Stat Block Snapshot:** AC 13, 20 HP. Can brew *Potions of Healing*.
* **Relationships:**
  * **Player Character:** Friendly vendor/supplier (offers 3 *Potions of Healing* upon arrival).
  * **Varnoth:** Friendly fellow quiet resident.
* **DM Roleplay Guide:** Soft-spoken, helpful, cautious about his dark past. Cold and clipped if pried about his tattoos.
