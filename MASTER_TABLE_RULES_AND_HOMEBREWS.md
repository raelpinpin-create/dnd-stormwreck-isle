### *Dragons of Stormwreck Isle & Mal'Goroth: The Weeping Planes*
> **Official Campaign Rulebook & DM Reference Guide**

## ⚡ 1. COMBAT INITIATION, SURPRISE & ABILITY CHECKS

### ⚔️ Intent to Attack & Initiative (Zero Free Pre-Combat Hits)
* **The Rule:** The moment any player or NPC declares an intent to attack (*"I cast Firebolt," "I shoot an arrow," "I stab him"*), **EVERYONE rolls Initiative immediately BEFORE the attack resolves**.
* **Attacking from Hidden / Stealth:**
  * Everyone **STILL rolls Initiative**.
  * If the attacker was successfully hidden (Stealth vs. Passive Perception) or caught the enemy completely off-guard:
    1. The target gets the **Surprised Condition** (their entire turn in Round 1 is skipped).
    2. The attacker gains **Advantage** on their opening attack roll.

### 🔍 Ability Checks in Combat Take a FULL ACTION
* **The Rule:** Making active ability checks during live combat (**Perception, Insight, Investigation, Persuasion, Arcana, etc.**) consumes your **FULL ACTION**.
* **Tactical Decision:** If an enemy starts chanting, shifting stances, or doing something suspicious, players must weigh whether it's worth burning their entire Action to roll Insight/Perception, or if they can deduce the enemy's tell and mechanics on their own from narrative clues without rolling.

## 🤝 2. THE HELP SYSTEM & HELP TOKENS

### 🎟️ Narrative Justification Requirement
* Each player character has **2 Help Tokens** maximum.
* **The Rule:** A player can expend 1 Help Token (as a Free Call-out within 30 ft / Line of Sight) to roll a **+1d6 Advantage Die** for an ally's d20 roll **ONLY IF they can provide a valid narrative explanation of HOW their character is actively helping**.
  * *Valid Examples:* Shouting an opening in the boss's guard, throwing sand/pebbles to distract eyes, casting light across a shadow, or physical positioning.
  * *Denied Tokens:* Spending a token with no narrative justification (*"I just use my token"*) will be **DENIED by the Lead DM**.
* **General Varnoth's Help Tokens:** The party can choose to spend General Varnoth's Help Tokens (1/2), but the party must still provide a valid narrative reason for how Varnoth intervenes.

## 💀 3. THE 0 HP, DYING & DEATH SAVE SYSTEM

### 🩸 Becoming 0 HP (The Dying / Unconscious Condition)
When a character drops to **0 HP**, they immediately fall **Prone and Unconscious** and enter the **Dying State**.

### 🛡️ How to Survive / Stabilize (DC 10 Death Save)
On each of your turns while Dying, roll a **DC 10 Death Saving Throw (d20)**:
1. **Roll 10+ (SUCCESS):** You immediately stabilize and wake up with **1 HP**! You are no longer dying, but remain in the **Prone position** on the floor (costs half movement to stand).
2. **Roll 1–9 (FAILURE):** You suffer **1 Death Strike**. 
3. **💥 2 FAILURES = PERMANENT DEATH:** If you fail **2 Death Saving Throws**, your character dies permanently! *(2 Strikes System)*.
4. **Natural 20:** Instantly regain **1 HP** and stand up immediately.

### 💖 Instant Cures
* **Healing Magic / Potions:** Receiving *any* healing (*Healing Word*, *Cure Wounds*, potion) immediately removes the Dying condition and restores positive HP. The character wakes up instantly (remains Prone until they stand on their turn).
* **Spare the Dying:** Instantly stabilizes the character at 0 HP (safe from dying, but still unconscious until healed).

### ⚔️ Taking Damage While Dying (The 2-Damage Lethal Rule)
* If an unconscious/dying character takes damage **2 separate times** (from enemies, environmental hazards, or accidental friendly fire) before being healed $\rightarrow$ **THE CHARACTER DIES PERMANENTLY**.

## 🧪 4. POTIONS, ELIXIRS & RESTING RULES

### 🏹 Throwing a Potion (Ranged Attack & Risk)
* **Action Cost:** Throwing a potion takes a **Full Action** and counts as an **Improvised Ranged Attack**.
* **Target DC:** The thrower must roll against a difficulty class of **DC 10 to DC 20** (depending on distance, obstacles, and cover).
* **Success (Hit):** The vial shatters near the ally; healing mist/liquid absorbs into them, restoring standard potion HP (`2d6 HP`) and waking them up!
* **Failure (Miss / Bad Throw):** The heavy glass vial strikes the dying ally directly—dealing **1d4 damage** and inflicting a **Damage Strike**, which can kill the downed player! *Throw wisely!*

### 🧪 Potion of Healing (Bonus Action: 2d6 + 1 HP)
* **Action Cost:** Drinking a *Potion of Healing* yourself takes a **Bonus Action** (Administering to an adjacent ally takes 1 Action).
* **Healing Amount:** Restores **`2d6 + 1 Hit Points`**!

### 🍷 Elixir of Life (Nerfed Post-Mortem Relic)
* **Revival Effect:** Administered to a creature that died within the last minute. The creature returns to life with **50% of its Maximum HP** (nerfed from 100%), and severed limbs knit back together.
* **Resurrection Sickness:** The revived character suffers a **-2 penalty to ALL d20 rolls** (attack rolls, saving throws, ability checks) until finishing their next **Long Rest**.

### ⛺ Short Rest Rules (2 Rests per Day Limit)
* The party is allowed a maximum of **2 Short Rests per day**.
* **Short Rest Healing:** Each Short Rest restores **25% of your Total Max HP** and regains **+1 Help Token** (capped at 2/2 max), in addition to class-specific short rest features (Channel Divinity, Pact Slots, Arcane Recovery).

## 💥 6. COMBAT MANEUVERS, ACTIONS & CRITICAL HITS

### 🎯 Brutal Critical Hits
* On a **Natural 20**, attacks deal **Maximum Base Weapon/Spell Damage + 1 Normal Damage Roll + Ability Modifiers** (e.g., a 1d6 weapon crit = `6 + 1d6 + modifier`).

### 🛡️ Tactical Actions
* **Dodge Action:** All attacks against you have **Disadvantage** (if you can see the attacker), and you gain **Advantage on Dexterity Saving Throws** until your next turn.
* **Disengage Action:** Your movement provokes **NO Opportunity Attacks** for the rest of your turn.
* **Ready Action:** Set a specific trigger and action/spell to execute as a Reaction.
* **Drop Prone (Free):** Dropping to the ground costs **0 ft of movement**. Ranged attacks from >5 ft have **Disadvantage** against you! (Costs half speed to stand up).

### 🥋 Combat Maneuvers (Replaces 1 Melee Attack)
* **Shove / Push (Athletics Contest):** Win the contest to either **Knock the target Prone** (giving melee allies Advantage) or **Push them 5 ft** (off cliffs, into water, off ships).
* **Grapple (Athletics Contest):** Sets enemy **Speed to 0**.
* **Non-Lethal Knockout:** When reducing a foe to 0 HP with a melee attack, the player can choose to knock them unconscious and stable at 0 HP.

## 🌊 7. ENVIRONMENTAL COMBAT HAZARDS

* ⚡ **Electrified Water / Puddles:** Lightning damage striking a creature standing in water arcs to all creatures in that water for **`1d4` extra Shock damage** and strips their reactions for 1 turn (Eflein's *Sculpt Spells* protects party allies).
* ❄️ **Shattered Ice:** Thunder damage or heavy bludgeoning impact on icy terrain violently shatters the surface, dealing **`1d4` Cold/Piercing Shrapnel damage** to all standing on the ice.

## 💰 8. SIMPLIFIED 2-COIN ECONOMY

* **10 Silver Pieces (SP) = 1 Gold Piece (GP)**. No copper pieces exist.
* Food, bread, lodging, and tavern supplies are priced in **SP**.
* Weapons, armor, adventuring gear, and magic items are priced in **GP**.
