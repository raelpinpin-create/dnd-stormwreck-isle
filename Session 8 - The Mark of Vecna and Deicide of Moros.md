# 🐉 Dragons of Stormwreck Isle — Session 8: The Mark of Vecna, The Deicide of Moros & The Return to Stormwreck Isle

> **Session Overview:**
> The party investigates the Shattered Obsidian Monolith, decoding the ancient holy mark of Vecna and uncovering Moros's stolen planar eye. After a deeply unsettling Long Rest under Moros's unblinking watch, the party ascends toward the Grand Cathedral across the Bridge of Ribs. Melkor awakens and makes a tragic, desperate sacrifice to shatter the bridge and stop Moros. Reaching the cathedral, the party faces the High Altar crossroads (Branch A: Multiverse Breach & Locust Swarm vs. Branch B: Pure Deicide Confrontation). Shattering Moros's Forehead Void Eye with *Lyra*, the party escapes through a collapsing planar vortex as General Varnoth heroically tackles and pins the dying god. Crashing back onto Toril, the heroes discover that months have passed—and Dragon's Rest has been brutally sacked by Sparkrender in search of the missing Bronze Scale!

### 🩸 BRANCH A: Father Flubs Touches the Altar (The Multiverse Breach)

If Flubs channels his divine life-force into the altar:

#### 1. The Cataclysmic Breach
> 📖 **Read to Players:**
> *"Flubs’s golden divine energy pours into the bone dais. But the moment it touches the stone, the golden light violently curdles into sickly violet and oily black. 
> 
> Moros throws his head back as his single cyclopean eye blazes like a dying sun. The ceiling of the cathedral tears open—not into the sky of Mal'Goroth, but into terrifying multiversal rifts! Through the tears, you see the glowing spires of the Astral Sea, the star-filled cosmos, and **the sunlit coast of the Sword Coast on Toril**!
> 
> Above you, thousands of cocoons split open. The **Null** drop down in a chittering rain!"*

#### 2. The Locust Mechanic (Fair & Playable)
* **The Swarm Ignores the Party:** The thousands of Null do **not** engage the heroes. Driven by pure cosmic hunger, they swarm up the cathedral walls and leap directly into the planar rifts to invade Toril and the multiverse like a plague of locusts.
* **The Objective:** Moros acts as the **Cosmic Anchor**. He levitates 15 ft above the altar, eyes locked open as he channels the tear. The party has **4 ROUNDS** to destroy his **Forehead Void Eye** or overload the altar before Toril is overrun!
* **Battlefield Hazards during Channeling:**
  * **Round 1:** *Gravitational Distortion* — Low gravity in the room; DC 13 Str Save at end of turn or drift 10 ft toward the ceiling.
  * **Round 2:** *Cosmic Lightning Arc* — Planar static arcs from the rifts (DC 13 Dex Save or take `2d8` Lightning/Force damage).
  * **Round 3:** *Falling Debris & Cocoons* — Falling carcasses crash into the arena (`2d6` Bludgeoning damage in a 10-ft radius, DC 12 Dex save to avoid).
  * **Round 4:** *Singularity Point* — The planar tear begins collapsing the cathedral.
* **The Implosion & Cosmic Vacuum:** 
  * When the Void Eye shatters, the planar conduit implodes violently.
  * The resulting reverse singularity creates a massive cosmic vacuum: **99% of the escaping Null are violently sucked back out of the Toril rift** and crushed into nothingness!
  * A single swirling blue vortex stabilizes for **1 round**—leading directly back to the flooded hold of the *Compass Rose*.
