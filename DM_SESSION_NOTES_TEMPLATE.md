# 🐉 Dragons of Stormwreck Isle — Session DM Notes Standard Template

> **Google Docs Styling Standard (MANDATORY FOR ALL SESSIONS):**
> * 📄 **Page Setup / Color:** `#FFFEFC` (Soft parchment / warm cream tint — RGB: `255, 254, 252`)
> * 🖋️ **Font Family:** **`Lora`** across the entire document
> * 📏 **Font Weights & Sizes:**
>   - **Header:** `Session [N] - [Title]` (HEADING_1, *Italic*, `Lora` 500)
>   - **Body Text:** `Lora` 500, `11pt`, Line Spacing `1.15` (`115%`)
>   - **Headings (Moments, Character Moments, Location Details, People, Plotlines, Cliffhanger):** HEADING_2, `Lora` 500
>   - **Bold Labels (`Outline:`, `Moment [N]:`, `Enemies:`, `Possible Loot:`, `If/then:`, `NPC Names:`):** `Lora` Bold 400
>   - **Notes Heading (`NOTES:`):** `22pt`, *Italic*, `Lora` 500
> * 🔘 **Bullet Hierarchy:**
>   - Level 0: Filled Round Bullet `●` (Indent Start: 36pt, First Line: 18pt)
>   - Level 1: Open Circle Bullet `○` (Indent Start: 72pt, First Line: 54pt)
>   - Level 2: Filled Square Bullet `■` (Indent Start: 108pt, First Line: 90pt)
>   - Notes list: Dash Bullet `-`

---

## 📜 Standard Document Content Structure

```text
[DOCUMENT HEADER: Session [N] - [Title]]

Outline: [1–2 sentences summarizing the session arc, party members, route/travel, and core objectives or discoveries.]

Moments
● Moment 1 ([Type / Title]): [1-sentence summary of the scene or initial encounter]
  ○ Enemies: [Enemy roster if combat / None if social]
  ○ Possible Loot: [Suggested trinkets, currency, ingredients, or relics]
  ○ If/then:
    ■ [Branch A: If players succeed on check / take path A -> Result A]
    ■ [Branch B: If players fail check / take path B -> Result B]

● Moment 2 ([Type / Title]): [Description of exploration, puzzle, or second encounter]
  ○ Enemies: [Enemy roster]
  ○ Possible Loot: [Loot items / potions]
  ○ If/then:
    ■ [If / Then logic]

● Moment 3 (Combat / Climax): [Description of climactic battle or pivotal decision]
  ○ Enemies: [Boss / mob roster]
  ○ Possible Loot: [Major treasure, scales, or magic items]
  ○ If/then:
    ■ [If / Then logic]

Character Moments
● [Player 1 / Eflein]: [Specific tactical highlight, spell choice, roleplay beat, or backstory cue]
● [Player 2 / New PC]: [Specific combat spotlight, discovery, or personal quest moment]
● General Varnoth: [Tactical aid, battlefield command, or advice]

Location Details
● [Location 1 Name]: [Visuals, sulfur/sea smells, ambient sounds, terrain hazards]
● [Location 2 Name]: [Visuals, acoustics, lighting, hidden alcoves]

People
● [NPC 1 Name]: [Role / Persona] | [Immediate motive, key dialogue, or state]
● [NPC 2 Name]: [Role / Persona] | [Lore revealed, trade items, or reaction]

Plotlines
● [Active Faction / Clocks]: [Current background threat progress, e.g. Sparkrender or Sharruth's Volcanic Fumes]
● [Dragon Scales Arc]: [Active clues or status of Metallic Scales]

Cliffhanger
[1–2 punchy sentences capturing the final scene transition, cliffhanger beat, or dramatic milestone before session ends]




NOTES:
- [Perception DC / Investigation DC notes]
- [Homebrew potion or ingredient crafting rules (e.g. Heartcap mushrooms, Ruby Morel)]
- [Tactical monster notes or environmental boons]
```
