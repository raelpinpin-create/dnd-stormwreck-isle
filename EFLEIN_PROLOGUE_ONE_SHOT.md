# 📜 EFLEIN’S PROLOGUE: THE EMPTY HEARTH
> **A Solo D&D 5e Theatre-of-the-Mind One-Shot & Character Origin**  
> *Setting:* Waterdeep (Scholar's District & Sub-Vaults) ➔ Cottage Backyard Shed  
> *Player Character:* **Eflein** (High Elf Evocation Wizard)  
> *Estimated Runtime:* ~3.0 Hours | *Format:* Theatre of the Mind (Zone-Based)

---

`
[0:00 - 0:35]  ACT I: The Empty Hearth (Cottage Mystery, Midnight Disappearance, 3 Urban Leads)
[0:35 - 1:10]  ACT II: The Forbidden Archives (Infiltration, Investigation, Celestial Puzzle)
[1:10 - 2:25]  ACT III: The Gauntlet of Sentinels (3-Stage Defense Gauntlet & Boss Duel)

## 🎲 DM SOLO PLAY RULES & MECHANICS

* **Solo Protection (Lianna’s Safeguard):** All automated defenses in this dungeon are programmed with non-lethal subdual protocols. Eflein cannot be permanently killed here—defeat knocks him out and teleports him safely to the library entrance.
* **Adrenaline / Help Tokens:** Give Eflein **2 Help Tokens** at the start of the session. He can spend a token to gain Advantage on any D20 roll (attack, save, or ability check) representing his desperate adrenaline.
* **Theatre of the Mind Zones:** Divide combat chambers into 3 simple distance zones:  
  `The Entry Arch` ➔ `The Chamber Center` ➔ `The Far Threshold`.  
  Moving between adjacent zones costs half normal movement.

# 🕯️ ACT I: THE EMPTY HEARTH (0:00 – 0:35)

### 🎙️ Opening Scene (Read Aloud to Player)
> *"Rain taps against the cottage window in a quiet, steady rhythm. The tallow candle on your desk has burned down to a pool of warm wax, illuminating old ink and parchment. The kettle on the hearth went cold two hours ago. It is past midnight—and Lianna’s chair across from yours remains empty. She always comes home before the cathedral bells toll twelve.*
> 
> *A sudden, frantic pounding rattles your front door."*

---

### 🔍 The Three Inquiries (Investigation Leads)

#### 1. Corin the Baker (The Frantic Witness)
* **Location:** At Eflein’s cottage door, flour on his apron, breathing heavily.
* **Information:** *"Eflein! Thank the gods you’re home. I saw Lianna by the High Market—she was moving fast, looking over her shoulder, clutching a scroll case. She was muttering about 'running out of time before the stars align.' She was heading straight toward the Scholar’s District."*
* **Reward:** Hands Eflein hot spiced tea (**+1 Help Token / Inspiration** for the night).

#### 2. Master Kaelen (The Cranky Bookbinder)
* **Location:** Bookbinder shop in the Scholar’s District, surrounded by leaning towers of parchment.
* **Dialogue:** *"Lianna was frantic. Demanded my finest tallow and charcoal. Kept muttering about ward seals and asking if the Grand Library’s restricted sub-level vault had been inspected this season."*
* **Skill Check:** **DC 11 Persuasion or Intimidation.** 
  * *Success:* Kaelen grumbles and hands over an *Archivist’s Skeleton Key* (Advantage on picking mundane locks in the library).

#### 3. Watch-Captain Vane (City Guard Patrol)
* **Location:** Standing near a street brazier outside the Grand Library.
* **Dialogue:** *"We were called to the Grand Library an hour ago—violet ward-fire flashed through the high stained glass. When we reached the doors, the wards burned our gauntlets. By the time the magic dissipated, whoever breached it was already sealed inside."*
* **Skill Check:** **DC 12 Insight or Arcana.**
  * *Success:* Eflein recognizes the violet ward-fire signature—it matches the defensive abjuration spells Lianna practiced at home in the evenings.

# 🏛️ ACT II: THE FORBIDDEN ARCHIVES (0:35 – 1:10)

### 🚪 Investigation & Infiltration
* **Atmosphere:** Pitch-black silence. The smell of ancient parchment, cedar, and ozone. Floating dust motes hang frozen in magical stasis.
* **Skill Check:** **DC 13 Investigation Check.**
  * *Success:* Eflein discovers faint violet glyph scuffs on the floorboards leading behind a revolving mahogany bookshelf into a hidden downward stone vault.
  * *Fail-Forward:* Eflein triggers a harmless static ward—his hair stands on end, but the hidden bookshelf grinds open on its own.

---

### 🧩 The Celestial Alignment Puzzle
A massive stone archway blocks the downward staircase, sealed by three rotating concentric astrological rings:
* **Outer Ring:** The Dragon (Constellation)
* **Middle Ring:** The Hourglass (Constellation)
* **Inner Ring:** The Star (Constellation)
* **Riddle Inscription:** Carved in Lianna’s neat handwriting along the stone border:  
  *"Time yields to duty; duty yields to the realm."*

#### The Solution:
1. *"Time yields to duty"* ➔ Rotate the **Hourglass** directly *beneath* the **Dragon**.
2. *"Duty yields to the realm"* ➔ Align the **Star** pointing directly toward the **North Keystone Pillar**.

#### The Reveal:
With a deep mechanical click, the archway snaps open, revealing a downward spiraling staircase carved from raw obsidian stone, radiating protective abjuration magic.

*(DM Fail-Safe: If the player gets stuck for more than 3 minutes, give a DC 10 History check to remember Lianna’s favorite star chart: she always drew the Hourglass rising under Draco on midsummer nights).*

# ⚔️ ACT III: THE GAUNTLET OF SENTINELS (1:10 – 2:25)

---

## 🗿 ROOM 1: The Perch of Watchers (Combat – 20 Mins)
* **Enemies:** 2 Arcane Sentry Gargoyles perched on fluted marble pillars.
* **Lore Twist:** The gargoyles do not fight to kill. They use non-lethal repelling bursts. A DC 11 Perception check reveals Lianna’s glyph on their chests: *"Repel intruders; preserve life."*

### 📋 STAT BLOCK: Arcane Sentry Gargoyle (×2)
*Medium Construct, Unaligned | CR 1/4 each (Solo Encounter)*
* **Armor Class:** 13 (Natural Armor)
* **Hit Points:** 16 (3d8 + 3)
* **Speed:** 25 ft., fly 30 ft.
* **STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 11 (+0) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 6 (-2) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 5 (-3)
* **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
* **Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
* **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned
* **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
* **Languages:** Understands Draconic/Celestial but cannot speak

**TRAITS:**
* **Lianna’s Safeguard:** All damage dealt by this creature is non-lethal subdual damage.
* **Guardian Inscription:** Carved with Lianna's signature violet protection glyph.

**ACTIONS:**
* **Stone Talons:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
  *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed 5 feet into the adjacent zone.
* **Repelling Force Pulse (Recharge 5–6):** The gargoyle emits a wave of violet force in a 15-foot cone.  
  Eflein must make a **DC 11 Strength saving throw** or be pushed 10 feet back and knocked **prone**. On a success, he is pushed 5 feet and does not fall prone.

---

## ❄️🔥 ROOM 2: The Core Chamber (Hazard & Combat – 25 Mins)
* **Enemy:** 1 Bound Mana Elemental anchored to a floating crystalline spire.
* **Environmental Count 20:** On Initiative Count 20, the floor runes pulse with condensed mana. Eflein must succeed on a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** or be pushed back to the *Entry Arch*.
* **Puzzle Bypass:** Eflein can defeat the elemental through combat OR use an action to make a **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check** to unweave the tethering rune. On a success, the elemental dissolves peacefully into glowing stardust.

### 📋 STAT BLOCK: Bound Mana Elemental
*Medium Elemental, Neutral | CR 1*
* **Armor Class:** 12
* **Hit Points:** 28 (5d8 + 5)
* **Speed:** 0 ft. (Tethered; 30 ft. hover radius around spire)
* **STR:** 10 (+0) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 8 (-1) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 7 (-2)
* **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Force
* **Damage Immunities:** Cold (Frost Form) OR Fire (Fire Form), Poison
* **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
* **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10

**ACTIONS:**
* **Arcane Mana Lash:** *Melee Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
  *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) cold or fire damage (non-lethal).
* **Frostfire Burst (Recharge 5–6):** 15-ft radius burst.  
  Eflein must make a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw**, taking 9 (2d6 + 2) cold or fire damage on a failed save, or half on a success.

---

## 👑 ROOM 3: The Aegis Warden (Boss Encounter – 30 Mins)
* **Enemy:** The Enchanted Runite Golem (Forged from woven mithral plates and purple crystal).

### 📋 STAT BLOCK: The Aegis Warden (Enchanted Runite Golem)
*Large Construct, Lawful Neutral | CR 2 (Solo Boss)*
* **Armor Class:** 14 (Runite Plating)
* **Hit Points:** 45 (6d10 + 12)
* **Speed:** 30 ft.
* **STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 8 (-1)
* **Saving Throws:** Str +4, Con +4
* **Damage Resistances:** Radiant, Necrotic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
* **Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
* **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
* **Senses:** Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11

**SPECIAL BOSS TRAITS:**
* **Mirror Aegis:** Whenever Eflein deals damage to the Golem with a spell, the Warden’s runic veins shift color, granting it **Resistance to that damage type** until the end of its next turn.
* **Hesitation of Recognition:** The Golem recognizes Eflein’s magical aura. If Eflein speaks to it, calls out Lianna’s name, or casts a spell she taught him, the golem’s eyes flicker purple, granting Eflein **Advantage on his next roll**.
* **Containment Protocol:** All attacks deal non-lethal damage.

**ACTIONS:**
* **Containment Slam:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
  *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning force damage, and Eflein is pushed 10 feet back.
* **Amethyst Repulsion Wave (Recharge 5–6):** The warden slams its fists into the floor.  
  Eflein must make a **DC 12 Strength saving throw**. On a failure, he takes 10 (3d6) force damage, is pushed 15 feet back, and falls **prone**. On a success, he takes half damage and is not knocked prone.

**REACTIONS:**
* **Aegis Ward (2/Day):** When hit by an attack, the Warden adds **+2 to its AC** against that attack, potentially turning a hit into a miss.

**CLIMAX DEFEAT TRIGGER (AT 0 HP):**
> Upon dropping to 0 HP, the golem drops to one knee. Its chest core pulses with a fading purple heartbeat as a soft, crystal-clear projection of **Lianna’s voice** echoes from within:
> 
> *"Intrusion contained... Eflein... if it is you... please... turn back."*
> 
> The golem crumbles into harmless, polished amethyst stones, unlocking the double doors ahead.

# 📖 ACT IV: THE INNER STUDY & THE TRUTH (2:25 – 3:00)

### 🕯️ The Discovery
The final doors part into a hidden sanctum built directly underneath the foundations of Eflein’s own home.
* An unfinished cup of porcelain tea sits on a cedar desk, cold but untouched.
* Centuried relics, Elven astrolabes, and planar maps line the bookshelves.
* In the center of the desk, beside a single dried jasmine flower, lies a sealed letter.

---

### 📜 Lianna’s Letter (Read Aloud to Player)

> *"Eflein,*
> 
> *If you are reading this, your brilliance solved the wards I spent a lifetime perfecting. I prayed with all my soul that you would never find this place.*
> 
> *I am not the simple scholar you fell in love with. For centuries, I have walked the Realms under the tutelage of Elminster himself, fighting horrors that remain unnamed in history books. When my time was done, I chose to bury my magic, to choose an ordinary life, and to choose you.*
> 
> *Last night, Elminster spoke into my dreams. A great cataclysm stirs across the cosmic balance, threatening the fragile existence of our world. He is calling every archmage of the old guard back to the front lines to unearth ancient power before it is too late.*
> 
> *I left in the dead of night because knowing the scope of this war will place a target on your back. Do not search for me. Live your life in the sun we built together.*
> 
> *With all my heart across every age,*  
> *— Lianna"*

---

### 🌅 The Final Narration (The Shed at Dawn)

> Beneath the letter lies a violet sigil carved into the cedar wood, humming with residual warmth. As your fingers brush the surface—
> 
> A blinding flash of purple light tears through the room.
> 
> In a split second, the flash drops you hard onto the dusty floorboards of your own backyard shed. The smell of potting soil, dry straw, and old cedar hits you instantly—a place so painfully familiar, right outside the quiet cottage you shared.
> 
> Dawn light peeks through the weathered planks, catching the dust floating in the air. In your shaking hands, you hold the letter. It still smells of her jasmine perfume, and the ache in your chest sharpens as the truth sinks in: the woman who loved you here was an immortal who walked away from the entire world just to hold your hand in the quiet.
> 
> You stare down at her handwriting, at the desperate words begging you to stay safe. She ran to protect you from a fight you were never meant to see... but an empty house without her isn't a life worth having.
> 
> You press the parchment tight against your chest, push open the creaking shed door, and step back inside only long enough to grab your cloak, your boots, and your spellbook. You lock the front door behind you, standing on the edge of the cobblestone road with your heart in your throat.
> 
> You are stepping into the open world for the very first time. You don't know where the war is, or what ancient power awaits... but you know you are not staying behind. And you will find her.
